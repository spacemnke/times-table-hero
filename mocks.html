Quest Land · art direction

Three visual styles

Same game, three different looks for the adventure land and its heroes. Mockups only — pick a direction (or mix).

1 · 8-Bit Arcaderetro · pixel
Bright NES-era pixel art. Chunky sprites, a punchy limited palette, blocky tiles and coin-boxes, subtle CRT scanlines. Nostalgic, playful, reads great on small screens.
2 · Game Boyretro · mono
Classic handheld, 4 shades of green. Dot-matrix monochrome with bold outlines. Super distinctive and cohesive; a strong "cool retro" identity, calmer and less busy.
3 · Synthwaveretro · neon
80s neon retro-futurism. Glowing outlines, a striped sunset sun and a perspective grid floor. Smooth (not pixel), high-energy and premium — the "wow" option.
Heroes shown: the same 3 characters (unicorn / cat / fox) would be drawn in whichever style you pick.
